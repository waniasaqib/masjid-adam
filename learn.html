The five daily prayersالصلوات الخمس

Muslims pray five times a day at times set by the movement of the sun. Each prayer is made up of units called rak‘ahs. On Fridays, the Jumu‘ah prayer takes the place of Dhuhr.

Upcoming Islamic datesالتقويم الهجري

The Islamic year follows the moon, so these dates are approximate until the new moon is sighted. Eid prayer times are announced at the masjid.
